# Site-specific build rules

- The public voice in this folder belongs only to `mineralrightspurchase.com`. Write as an Oklahoma City mineral-purchase closing-table teacher: patient, chronological, glossary-driven, and exact about the ownership decimal, deed exhibit, title evidence, curative items, settlement math, signatures, funding, recording, payor notice, and post-closing transfer. Never turn this voice into a reusable voice pack and never copy it into another project.
- The homepage hero must always carry one visible, SEO-driven H1 that names the core service and Oklahoma City market. Never ship an image-only or blank hero.
- Public business and educational copy must never use singular first-person `I`, `me`, or `my`; write in the company voice or address the owner directly as `you`.
- Preserve the captured Eastern Shipbuilding DOM, five-slide photographic hero cadence, blue-and-white section rhythm, news feed, viewing portal, nine-line process band, image feature, internal navigation, five-entry timeline, promise columns, listing feed, article shell, typography, colors, spacing, and responsive behavior. Relabel donor elements in place; do not substitute a generic local-SEO template.
- Every public image must come from this project's mapped image inventory. Remove donor imagery, tracking pixels, 1-by-1 icons, miniature thumbnails used as decoration, footer logos, social icons, decorative image glitches, and accidental media fragments. A taxonomy slug page may contain exactly one raster image reference: its `/ours/` hero image. Related sections and closing CTAs must use typography, color, spacing, and CSS geometry rather than extra imagery.
- Taxonomy slug pages must keep the article readable across the full desktop composition, include a donor-styled related-guides section, and end with a clear contact and phone CTA. Do not squeeze long copy into a narrow side column.
- The desktop header must show the assigned phone number and a visually distinct link to `#contact-form` on the Contact page. Consolidate secondary navigation into the accessible Explore dropdown before the row can crowd or overlap; switch the primary navigation to the existing menu at narrower desktop widths.
- Every public contact form must contain exactly four visitor fields, in this order: Name, Email, Phone, Message. The rendered field names must be `name`, `email`, `phone`, and `message`, and the canonical handler must accept those keys.
- The street address may exist only in metadata and inside Google Maps iframe queries. It must never render as visible text. Every page-level footer must contain exactly one Google Maps embed and no other media. The contact page may additionally contain one unlabeled, marked Google Maps embed in the contact body.
- Every footer must contain zero images, pictures, SVGs, videos, canvases, or source elements. Its only permitted iframe is the single Google Maps embed.
- Do not claim completion until the shared verifier, address/footer/map compliance gate, miniature-image regression scan, screenshot pass, and full-resolution donor-versus-built review all pass.
