# Site-specific build rules

- The public voice in this folder belongs only to `mineralrightspurchase.com`. Write as an Oklahoma City mineral-purchase closing-table teacher: patient, chronological, glossary-driven, and exact about the ownership decimal, deed exhibit, title evidence, curative items, settlement math, signatures, funding, recording, payor notice, and post-closing transfer. Never turn this voice into a reusable voice pack and never copy it into another project.
- The homepage hero must always carry one visible, SEO-driven H1 that names the core service and Oklahoma City market. Never ship an image-only or blank hero.
- Public business and educational copy must never use singular first-person `I`, `me`, or `my`; write in the company voice or address the owner directly as `you`.
- Preserve the captured Eastern Shipbuilding DOM, five-slide photographic hero cadence, blue-and-white section rhythm, news feed, viewing portal, nine-line process band, image feature, internal navigation, five-entry timeline, promise columns, listing feed, article shell, typography, colors, spacing, and responsive behavior. Relabel donor elements in place; do not substitute a generic local-SEO template.
- Every public image must come from this project's mapped image inventory. Remove donor imagery, tracking pixels, 1-by-1 icons, miniature thumbnails used as decoration, footer logos, social icons, decorative image glitches, and accidental media fragments.
- The street address may exist only in metadata and inside the Google Maps iframe query. It must never render as visible text. The contact page must contain exactly one Google Maps embed.
- Every footer must contain zero images, pictures, SVGs, videos, canvases, iframes, or source elements.
- Do not claim completion until the shared verifier, address/footer/map compliance gate, miniature-image regression scan, screenshot pass, and full-resolution donor-versus-built review all pass.
